Chun-Yi Lee

Data Scientist · Computational Neuroscientist

National Taiwan University & Academia Sinica · Taipei, Taiwan

I take methods built for high-dimensional, noisy, multimodal data in neuroscience and apply them to manufacturing, finance and language: citation-grounded RAG, multi-agent LLM systems, Chinese conversation analytics, computer-vision feature extraction and semiconductor yield prediction. I have also shipped a production system end to end, from backend to deployment: the registration and payment platform for an international conference. Ten years of fMRI and behavioral research taught me to evaluate rigorously on noisy data. From 2024 to 2025 I spent nearly a year in Boston as a visiting scholar at Brandeis University, funded by Taiwan's Ministry of Education, working daily in English with international teams.

I am a PhD candidate in Interdisciplinary Neuroscience at National Taiwan University and Academia Sinica, graduating in March 2027, and I am looking for data science, machine learning and AI engineering roles in Taiwan.
